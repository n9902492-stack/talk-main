## Brand & Style

The design system powers an elevated, high-engagement video meeting and webinar platform engineered for teams who demand professional production quality without technical friction. Evolved from modern web conferencing tools, it rejects the sterile, utilitarian gray-blue chrome of legacy meeting clients in favor of a warm, luminous, and editorial product atmosphere.

### Personality & Emotional Resonance
- **Radiant & Hospitable:** Warm pastel yellow gradients and golden amber accents create an inviting, collaborative stage that eases meeting fatigue and sparks engagement.
- **Precision Modernism:** Crisp neo-grotesque typography paired with razor-sharp alignment and deliberate whitespace signals enterprise-grade reliability and high-throughput stability.
- **Tactile Softness:** Generous corner radii (`rounded-2xl` and `rounded-3xl`), pill badges, and elevated card surfaces deliver approachable luxury.

### Design Movement
The style blends **Warm Editorial Minimalism** with **Refined Tactile SaaS**. It avoids harsh neo-brutalist lines and over-saturated dark voids, favoring clean bright paper surfaces (`#FFFFFF`), whisper-soft structural containment lines (`#E8E8E8`), and creamy ambient surfaces (`#F8F9FA`) that showcase live video streams, interactive agendas, and product demos.

## Colors

The palette establishes an uplifting, sunlit aesthetic anchored by sharp charcoal typography and warm gold interactions.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Accent (`#D4A017` - Golden Yellow):** The operational driver for high-intent actions, key badges, active indicators, toggle states, and focus triggers. Paired with dark charcoal text or pure white text depending on container depth.
- **Secondary (`#171717` - Deep Charcoal):** Provides deep, authoritative grounding for all structural headers, dominant CTAs, and meeting controls.
- **Tertiary Surface Wash (`#FFF0BD` to `#FFF8E4`):** A gentle gradient spectrum applied across hero stages, presentation backdrops, spotlight panels, and notification containers.
- **Neutral Foreground (`#646464` - Slate Secondary):** Neutralizes visual clutter, rendering body descriptions, disabled indicators, tab titles, and metadata tags with optical clarity.
- **Card Surface (`#F8F9FA`):** An off-white canvas tier that lifts cards, attendee tiles, toolbars, and agenda lists off the pure white base.
- **Border Subtle (`#E8E8E8`):** Defines clean containment boundaries without high-contrast friction.
- **Canvas Base (`#FFFFFF`):** The clean white foundation for the entire core application shell and documentation surfaces.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the friendly geometric posture of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headlines and branding with the systematic clarity of **Inter** for dense UI, chat, participant rosters, and agenda interactions.

### Hierarchy & Typesetting Rules
- **Display & Headings:** Rendered exclusively in Plus Jakarta Sans with negative letter tracking (`-0.03em` to `-0.015em`) and high contrast `#171717`. They deliver an authoritative, polished presence that retains humanity.
- **Body & Longform:** Inter provides neutral, open counters and high legibility across dynamic video overlays, speaker transcripts, and interactive chat streams.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Pill badges, timestamps, microphone states, and participant tags use tight, uppercase or semi-bold Inter to ensure legibility at micro scales.

## Layout & Spacing

The system is constructed on an adaptable 8pt geometric grid with fluid 12-column containers on desktop and 4-column structures on mobile.

### Layout Principles
- **Hero & Presentation Canvas:** Centers content within a max-width envelope of 1280px. Hero stages utilize generous vertical padding (`space-2xl` to `5rem`) combined with smooth gradient backgrounds that flow seamlessly into clean white content segments.
- **Meeting Room Stage:** Fluid 100vw/100vh application viewport featuring dynamic sidebar panels (Chat, Agenda, Participants) that collapse gracefully on viewports smaller than 1024px.
- **Spacing Cadence:** 
  - Micro-gaps between controls and avatar chips: `space-xs` (4px) to `space-sm` (8px).
  - Internal card padding: `space-lg` (24px) for desktop, `space-md` (16px) for mobile.
  - Section-to-section separation: `space-xl` (40px) to `space-2xl` (64px).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy combines warm ambient shadows with crisp 1px borders (`#E8E8E8`), avoiding heavy drops in favor of lightweight spatial tiers.

### Depth Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** Pure background surfaces (`#FFFFFF`) and hero gradient planes.
- **Level 1 (Subtle Inset / Base Card):** Surface `#F8F9FA` bounded by a 1px solid `#E8E8E8` border. No drop shadow. Used for passive cards, attendee list rows, and agenda blocks.
- **Level 2 (Elevated Product Card):** Surface `#FFFFFF` or `#F8F9FA`, 1px solid `#E8E8E8`, supported by an ambient drop shadow: `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(23, 23, 23, 0.06), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(23, 23, 23, 0.03)`. Used for interactive feature previews, video tiles, and pricing tables.
- **Level 3 (Floating Controls & Overlays):** Meeting control dock, live reactions popovers, and sticky navigation headers. Layered with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` over a semi-transparent base (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`), finished with `0 20px 40px -12px rgba(23, 23, 23, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary uses sweeping, soft geometry to communicate warmth, modern ease, and tactile polish.

### Shape Tiers
- **Interactive Micro Elements:** Buttons, text fields, and segmented control chips use `rounded-xl` (12px) to `rounded-2xl` (16px).
- **Cards & Video Panels:** Feature cards, screen share previews, and attendee video feeds apply `rounded-2xl` (16px) or `rounded-3xl` (24px).
- **Hero Banners & Window Frames:** High-level hero product containers employ `rounded-3xl` (24px to 32px) for an elevated showcase effect.
- **Badges & Docks:** Category badges, meeting status indicators, and audio/video floating toolbars use fully rounded pill shapes (`border-radius: 9999px`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Deep Charcoal (`#171717`) or Golden Accent (`#D4A017`) with crisp white or charcoal text, `rounded-xl` (12px), `space-md` horizontal padding, height 44px (48px for hero CTAs). Active/hover states feature a subtle scale transform (`scale(1.02)`) and luminance lift.
- **Secondary Action:** White surface (`#FFFFFF`), 1px border `#E8E8E8`, text `#171717`. Hover states transition to `#F8F9FA` with border tint `#D4A017`.
- **Ghost / Control Button:** Transparent base, text `#646464`, hovering reveals a soft golden yellow tint (`#FFF8E4`) with `#171717` text.

### Badges & Chips
- **Announcement Pill Badge:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), soft pastel background (`#FFF0BD`), border `1px solid rgba(212, 160, 23, 0.3)`, text `#171717`, font `label-md`. Contains optional icon or micro avatar.
- **Filter / Segmented Chips:** Pill-shaped, base background `#F8F9FA` with `#646464` text. Selected state switches to `#171717` with `#FFFFFF` text.

### Cards & Product Previews
- **Feature Card:** Surface `#F8F9FA`, border `1px solid #E8E8E8`, radius `rounded-2xl` (20px), padding `space-lg`. Internal media elements sit on clean white inset frames with soft ambient shadows.
- **Product Preview Card:** Hero window container with radius `rounded-3xl` (24px), elevated by Level 2 shadows, housing mock video feeds, active agenda rails, and floating participant badges.

### Form Inputs & Controls
- **Text Fields:** Height 44px, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E8E8E8`, radius `rounded-xl` (12px), text `#171717`, placeholder `#646464`. Focused state introduces a 2px outline in `#D4A017` with zero border offset.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Radius 6px (circular for radio), border `1.5px solid #E8E8E8`. Checked state fills with `#D4A017` with white checkmark glyph.

### Meeting Bar & Control Dock
- **Floating Meeting Toolbar:** Centered floating dock, `rounded-full`, backdrop blur `16px`, background `rgba(23, 23, 23, 0.92)` or `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)` with contrasting icons for mute, video toggle, screen share, reactions, and leave room.
